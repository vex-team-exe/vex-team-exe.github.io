Our Story

Defined by Design.

For five years, our members competed with Simon Langton Grammar School for Boys as 23218X Fractal and 23218Y Nemesis. We have won multiple awards at regional and national level, placed top 5 in the UK on multiple occasions, and qualified for the World Championship in 2026.

This season we have gone independent, with no school affiliation. We are six of the most committed and competitive members of that programme, working to build our futures and compete at the highest level in VEX — the largest robotics competition in the world.

We are Team .exe: independent, self-directed, and entirely committed to qualifying for the VEX Robotics World Championship in 2027.
